# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
ThetaIDE a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best for the project as a whole, not just for us

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

* Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, its
issues and pull requests, and the project's chat channels — and also applies
when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Review and Collaboration

Because much of this project moves through pull requests, a note on how review
is expected to work here:

* Review the code, not the author. "This loses the run id" rather than "you
  forgot the run id."
* A review that says only "LGTM" on a large change is not a review.
* Disagreement is expected and useful. Settle it with evidence — a failing
  test, a measurement, a link to the spec — rather than by seniority.
* If a change is urgent and no reviewer is available, say so in the pull
  request and merge it; do not quietly bypass review and leave no record.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project maintainers through a GitHub issue, or privately to
any maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter
of any incident, and have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that are not
aligned to this Code of Conduct.
